Public Telegram channels,
as JSON.

TelegramMe reads the public web previews of Telegram channels (t.me/s/…) and serves them as a REST and GraphQL API: channel profiles, posts with media, polls and reactions, pagination and ranked feeds across channels. No Telegram account or API key.

Interactive Swagger UI for the REST API: try every endpoint from the browser. The schema is at /openapi.json.

GraphQL

One query for a channel, its posts and previews of others, in the GraphiQL IDE at /graphql.

Endpoints

MethodPathReturns
GET/v1/body/{channel}Profile, latest posts and pagination
GET/v1/more/{channel}/{before|after}/{position}Older or newer posts
GET/v1/post/{channel}/{id}One post
GET/v1/preview/{channel}Short channel summary
POST/v1/previewsUp to 100 previews at once
POST/v1/feedRanked posts across channels
POST/graphqlAll of the above in one query
